Frequently Asked Questions

How does the virtual try-on work?

Our technology uses augmented reality and computer vision to map clothing items onto your body in real-time. You can use your camera or upload a photo to see how items will look on you.

Is the virtual try-on accurate?

Our AI has been trained on thousands of body types and clothing items to provide the most accurate representation possible. While not 100% perfect, our users report over 90% satisfaction with fit accuracy.

What devices support virtual try-on?

Our technology works on most modern smartphones, tablets, and computers with cameras. For the best experience, we recommend using Chrome or Safari browsers on devices less than 3 years old.

Is my privacy protected?

Absolutely. We don't store your images permanently and all processing happens securely on our servers. Your data is never shared with third parties.
